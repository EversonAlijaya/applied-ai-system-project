# Senior and Puppy Care

- Senior pets often need extra care tasks, such as medication reminders and gentler, shorter activity, and these are usually high priority.
- Puppies need frequent bathroom breaks, roughly every two to three hours while awake, plus short training sessions.
- Puppy training sessions should be short, about 5 to 10 minutes, because young dogs have short attention spans.
- Senior dogs may need help with mobility and should avoid long or strenuous exercise.
- Medication or health-related tasks for senior pets should be treated as high priority and scheduled at consistent times.
- Puppies sleep a lot, often 18 to 20 hours a day, so care tasks should fit around their rest.
- Socializing a puppy gently with new people, sounds, and other animals is important in its first few months.
- Senior pets should see a vet more often, ideally twice a year, to catch age-related problems early.
- Both puppies and senior pets are more sensitive to heat and cold, so plan outdoor time carefully.
