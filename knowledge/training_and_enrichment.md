# Training and Enrichment

- Short, positive training sessions of 5 to 10 minutes work better than long ones, especially for young animals.
- Reward-based training, using treats or praise, is more effective and humane than punishment.
- Mental enrichment, such as puzzle feeders and new toys, reduces boredom and problem behavior.
- Dogs benefit from daily practice of basic commands like sit, stay, and come.
- Rotating toys every week keeps pets more interested than leaving all toys out at once.
- Enrichment is especially important for indoor pets and high-energy breeds that cannot burn energy outdoors.
