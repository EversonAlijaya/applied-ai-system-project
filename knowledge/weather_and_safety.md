# Weather and Safety

- In hot weather, walk dogs in the early morning or evening and avoid the midday heat.
- Never leave a pet in a parked car, even for a few minutes, because temperatures rise dangerously fast.
- Hot pavement can burn paws; if it is too hot for the back of your hand, it is too hot for paws.
- In cold weather, short-haired and small dogs may need a coat, and outdoor time should be limited.
- Provide shade and extra water for pets that spend time outside in summer.
- Watch for signs of overheating in pets, such as heavy panting, drooling, and weakness, and cool them down right away.
