# Dog Walking Guidance

- Adult dogs generally need 30 to 60 minutes of walking or active exercise each day, often split into two walks.
- Puppies need about 5 minutes of walking per month of age, up to twice a day. For example, a 3-month-old puppy should walk about 15 minutes at a time.
- Senior dogs benefit from shorter, gentler walks, usually around 15 to 20 minutes, to protect aging joints.
- High-energy breeds (such as border collies or huskies) often need more than 60 minutes of exercise per day.
- Walking is best scheduled in the cooler parts of the day, such as early morning and evening, especially in hot weather.
