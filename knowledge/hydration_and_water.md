# Hydration and Water

- Fresh, clean water should be available to pets at all times, and bowls should be refilled at least once a day.
- A rough guide is that dogs need about one ounce of water per pound of body weight each day.
- Wash water bowls daily to prevent bacteria and slime from building up.
- Pets need more water in hot weather or after exercise, so keep extra water available on warm days.
- Signs of dehydration include dry gums, tiredness, and loss of skin elasticity; these need prompt attention.
- Cats often drink too little, so wet food and water fountains can help keep them hydrated.
