# Health and Vet Signs

- Adult pets should have a routine vet checkup at least once a year; senior pets benefit from twice a year.
- Keep vaccinations and flea, tick, and worm treatments up to date on a regular schedule.
- Warning signs that need a vet soon include not eating, vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, or trouble breathing.
- Sudden changes in weight, drinking, or bathroom habits can signal a health problem.
- Medication should be given at consistent times, and these tasks are high priority.
- Keep a record of your pet's weight, medications, and vet visits to track their health over time.
