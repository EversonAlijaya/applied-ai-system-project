# Cat Care Guidance

- Litter boxes should be scooped at least once a day to keep cats comfortable and prevent them from avoiding the box. Scooping takes about 5 minutes.
- Cats need daily play and enrichment, around 10 to 15 minutes, to stay healthy and reduce boredom.
- Most adult cats are fed twice a day, and fresh water should always be available.
- Indoor cats benefit from scratching posts and climbing spaces to support natural behavior.
- Unlike dogs, cats do not need to be walked, so care tasks focus on feeding, play, litter, and grooming.
