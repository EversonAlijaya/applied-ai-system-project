# Feeding Guidance

- Most adult dogs should be fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart (for example, morning and evening).
- Puppies need more frequent meals, usually three to four times a day, because they have small stomachs and high energy needs.
- Most adult cats do well with two meals a day, though some owners use several smaller portions.
- Fresh water should be available at all times. Check and refill water bowls at least once daily.
- Feeding at consistent times each day helps digestion and makes routines easier to follow. A typical feeding task takes about 5 to 10 minutes.
